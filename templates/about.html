About StartupIQ

Data Mining & Business Intelligence Final Year Microproject

StartupIQ – Startup Funding Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end Data Mining & Business Intelligence application that collects, stores, cleans, mines, and visualises global startup funding data to generate investment insights.

Final Year Microproject DMBI Domain Engineering Application
Project Objective

Build a complete web-based decision support system that: collects startup data via web scraping, stores it in a normalised MySQL relational database, performs data preprocessing and feature engineering, applies text mining (TF-IDF, sentiment analysis), executes data mining (K-Means clustering, Apriori association rules), and presents interactive business intelligence through a Plotly-powered executive dashboard.
