Risk Profiling Methodology
Clustering (K-Means)

Startups segmented into 4 strategic clusters using funding magnitude, rounds, age, and velocity. Each cluster receives a derived risk profile.

Failure Probability Score

Score = 1 − (total_funding / max_funding) for active startups. Closed startups receive 0.80–0.95. Acquired/IPO receive 0.05–0.15.

Risk Interpretation

>60% → High Risk (underfunded, early-stage)
30–60% → Medium Risk (moderate traction)
<30% → Low Risk (well-funded, scaling)
